Museum AI usability questionnaire

Complete this questionnaire after using the XJTLU Museum AI system. There are no right or wrong answers. Your responses are anonymous.

The study evaluates four Museum AI tasks: starting the guide, asking about an exhibit, navigating to the next stop, and recovering from an unclear answer. The questions examine task difficulty, exhibit information, route guidance, trust, and recovery.
