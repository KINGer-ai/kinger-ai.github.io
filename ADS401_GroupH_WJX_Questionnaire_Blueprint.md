## Project theme

Course theme: Evaluating AI Integration at XJTLU.

Study title: Usability Evaluation of the XJTLU Museum AI System.

Target users: XJTLU students and staff aged 18+ with mixed digital confidence and prior museum experience.

## Question structure

1. Role at XJTLU.
2. Prior use of the XJTLU Museum AI System.
3. Task completion matrix for four tasks (independent, assisted, failed).
4. Task ease matrix (1-7).
5. Task confidence matrix (1-7).
6. Museum AI experience matrix (start clarity, content relevance, navigation, recovery, trust, cognitive load).
7. Standard SUS matrix (10 items, 1-5).
8. Main obstacle.
9. Open feedback on useful, unclear, or missing exhibit information.
10. Most important content or interaction improvement.

## Research alignment

- RQ1 task success: Question 3 plus moderator observation.
- RQ2 usability and efficiency: Questions 4-7 plus observation time and errors.
- RQ3 barriers: Questions 8-10 plus think-aloud notes.

## Statistical alignment

- H1: Per-task independent success against 80%, using a binomial test.
- H2: SUS against the benchmark of 68, using a t-test or Wilcoxon based on distribution.
- H3: Task ease and confidence against SUS, using exploratory Spearman correlations.
- H4: Obstacle categories and open responses, using thematic coding.
- Quality checks: Missingness, duplicate IDs, SUS internal consistency, and distribution checks.
