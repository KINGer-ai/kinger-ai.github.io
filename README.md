# ADS401 Group H Project

Single-page web report and final statistical materials for:

**Museum AI Guide and Post-Visit Experience**

Course theme: **Evaluating AI Integration at XJTLU**

## Research Question

How do post-visit experience ratings differ between students who used the
museum's AI guide and students who toured without it?

## Final Dataset

- 30 XJTLU students
- AI-guide group: 15
- No-AI comparison group: 15
- 17 undergraduates and 13 postgraduates
- Q7-Q15: nine post-visit experience ratings on a 1-5 scale
- 270 of 270 valid item ratings
- No missing or out-of-range values

## Analysis

- Two-sided independent-samples Mann-Whitney U test for each Q7-Q15 item
- Holm correction across nine exploratory comparisons
- Rank-biserial correlation for effect direction and magnitude
- No raw or Holm-adjusted p-value below .05
- The result is absence of evidence for a difference in this sample, not proof
  that the two conditions are equivalent

## Important Design Boundary

The team planned to alternate groups by arrival order, but the recorded group
sequence is not strictly alternating. The study is not described as randomized,
and the group comparison is not interpreted as a causal effect of the AI guide.
