Research question and visit design

The study asks whether the two tour groups differ across the same nine post-visit measures. It does not ask participants for open-ended explanations.

Research question

Do post-visit experience ratings differ between students who used the XJTLU Museum AI guide and students who toured without it?

Each participant rated engagement, interest, learning, understanding, reflection, meaning, context, emotion, and theme resonance on a 1-5 scale.

    Consent

  2. 02
    Tour condition

    AI-guided tour or labels-only visit in the same exhibition.

  3. 03

  4. 04

Why this setting

A real museum trial

The XJTLU Museum has used digital and AI-supported exhibits to create new visitor interactions. This study keeps the evaluation tied to one museum context and one short visit.

Participant data and statistical method

All 30 participants had complete Q7-Q15 ratings. The analysis treats each item as ordinal and corrects for nine simultaneous comparisons.

Mann-Whitney U

A two-sided independent-samples test compares the rank distributions of the two groups for every item.

Holm correction

The correction limits false positives across the nine related tests. Every adjusted p-value is 1.000.

Effect direction

Signed Z values describe direction. Positive values favour the no-AI group; negative values favour the AI group.

Read the result

Absence of evidence is not equivalence

No item has a raw or adjusted p-value below .05. The sample does not support a claim that the AI guide changed the nine ratings, but it also cannot prove that the two conditions were equivalent.

Interpretation and study limits

The result is useful because it sets a clear boundary for what this sample can support and what the next study should improve.

What the data show

The AI group has higher descriptive means on five items and the no-AI group on four. None of these differences survives correction for nine comparisons.

The current sample is too small to rule out modest effects, and the allocation record does not support a causal claim about the AI guide.

What the next study needs

  • A verified allocation procedure before each session.
  • One pre-specified primary outcome.
  • A larger sample with comparable visit routes and timing.
  • Task success or time-on-task only if the design is ethically approved.
