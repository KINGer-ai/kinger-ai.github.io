## Theme Check

The work directly addresses the module theme, **Evaluating AI Integration at XJTLU**, through one bounded institutional AI system and four visitor tasks. It does not drift into a general survey about attitudes toward AI.

## Statistical Check

- H1 uses a per-task binomial test against 0.80.
- The four tasks are not pooled because repeated observations within participants are dependent.
- H2 uses a one-sample t-test or Wilcoxon signed-rank test against 68.
- SUS internal consistency is reported with Cronbach's alpha and a small-sample caution.
- H3 uses exploratory Spearman correlations between task ratings and SUS.
- H4 uses thematic coding of observation notes and open questionnaire responses.
- Missingness, duplicate IDs, outliers, and normality are checked before interpretation.
